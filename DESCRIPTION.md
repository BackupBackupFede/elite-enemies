Sometimes a hostile mob spawns as an elite. It's tougher, it carries a named item with over-the-limit enchantments, and it drops that item when it dies.

Server-side only. Players can join with a vanilla client.

| Rank | Health | Example drop |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Common | ×1.5 | Knockback Stick (Knockback V) |
| ★★ Uncommon | ×1.9 | Apocalypse (Sharpness VI, Smite VI) |
| ★★★ Rare | ×2.8 | Longshot (Power VIII) |
| ★★★★ Legendary | ×3.7 | Kingslayer (Sharpness X) |

- Elites show a boss bar. Rare and legendary ones are also announced in chat.
- Mobs from spawners and spawn eggs never become elites.
- `/eliteenemies settings` opens a menu to change settings. Operators only.
- Everything else, including which mobs can become elites, is in `config/eliteenemies.json`.
